Guide

🧪 Using 7-in-1 test strips well
  • Keep the bottle tightly closed, dry fingers only, never touch the pads.
  • Dip 1–2 seconds elbow-deep, remove with pads UP, hold level ~15 s. Don't shake or wipe.
  • Read within 30–60 s — colors keep drifting after that.
  • Test at the same time of day (morning, before sun burns off chlorine) for comparable trends.
  • Strips expire ~1–2 years; a bottle open all season reads progressively worse.
🌀 Sand filter: backwash step-by-step
  1. Pump OFF. Never move the multiport valve with the pump running.
  2. Valve to BACKWASH. Pump ON, run 2–3 min until sight-glass water runs clear.
  3. Pump OFF. Valve to RINSE. Pump ON 30–60 s (re-settles the sand bed).
  4. Pump OFF. Valve back to FILTER. Pump ON. Note the clean-filter pressure.
  5. Top up the pool — backwash costs water. Backwash when pressure rises ~0.3 bar (5 psi) over the clean baseline, or on your schedule.
  6. Sand lasts ~5 years; channeling / constant cloudiness despite good chemistry means change it.
🧽 Cartridge filter cleaning
  1. Pump OFF, release pressure, open housing, remove cartridge.
  2. Hose every pleat top-to-bottom (garden hose, not pressure washer — it tears the fabric).
  3. Monthly-ish: soak overnight in filter cleaner (or 1:10 dishwasher-detergent solution), rinse well.
  4. Greasy film / sunscreen season: degrease first — acid-washing a greasy cartridge sets the grease.
  5. Keep a second cartridge to rotate. Replace when pleats stay grey or fabric frays.
⚖️ Water balance in one minute
  • Order of operations: alkalinity → pH → chlorine → stabilizer → hardness.
  • TA is the pH shock-absorber. Low TA = pH bounces; high TA = pH creeps up.
  • pH 7.2–7.6: above 7.8 chlorine is barely working even if the test shows plenty.
  • CYA (stabilizer) protects chlorine from the sun, but too much locks it up — 30–50 ppm outdoor.
  • Add one chemical at a time, pump running, retest after 4–6 h. Small steps beat big swings.
💥 When and how to shock
  • Shock after: parties/heavy use, heavy rain, algae, combined chlorine ≥ 0.5 ppm, foul smell.
  • Dose to reach breakpoint: ~10× combined chlorine, at dusk, pump running overnight.
  • Green pool: brush first, shock to high FC, filter 24/7, backwash daily, repeat until FC holds overnight.
  • Don't swim until FC is back below 5 ppm.
⏱ Pump & circulation
  • Rule of thumb: the whole pool volume through the filter 1–2× per day.
  • Skimmer flap moving, return jets angled slightly down & sideways to spin the water.
  • Run the pump when dosing and during the day in algae season (chlorine + sun + circulation).
